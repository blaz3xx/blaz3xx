### Featured projects

#### 🔥 [Polissya Wildfire Risk](https://github.com/blaz3xx/polissya-wildfire-risk)

A day-ahead wildfire risk forecasting system for the Ukrainian Polissya forest belt, built under **Erasmus+ EcoMinds**. Ten years of ERA5 weather reanalysis joined with NASA FIRMS satellite fire detections, clipped to an OpenStreetMap forest mask. A bidirectional LSTM over a 14-day window reaches **ROC-AUC 0.877 and recall 0.922** on a chronological hold-out set, beating logistic regression, random forest, and CatBoost baselines. Outputs feed a three-tier early warning system with live daily inference.

`Python` `TensorFlow` `scikit-learn` `CatBoost` `GeoPandas` `NASA FIRMS` `Open-Meteo`

#### 📱 Roley — job matching mobile app
